# Choices made on purpose

Some parts of VWB may look like something is missing. Many of these are choices, made to keep
people safe. Please do not change them without opening an issue first to talk it through.

## Safety and privacy

- **No GPS.** The app never asks where a phone is. It only uses the middle of a zip code or town.
- **No photos of people.** People show as drawn avatars. The only picture upload is one entrance photo
  for an event, with no people in it, added by leaders of approved groups.
- **No face or fingerprint sign in.** The second step is an authenticator code.
- **No message archive.** VWB does not keep copies of chats. VWB admins cannot read village chats,
  Campfires or Cottage Chats.
- **Quick exit works the way it does on purpose.** It lets someone leave the app fast if they are not safe.
  Talk it through in an issue before you change it.
- **Public needs show what is needed, never who or where.** A post never says who asked or where to deliver.
- **Emails stay hidden.** The app shows an email only when a group chose to show its contact email.

## Trust and fairness

- **New members have limits for 14 days,** unless someone vouches for them or they finish a task with someone.
- **Removing someone waits 24 hours.** This cooling-off time stops a removal made in anger or by mistake.
- **Emergency crews are called only after someone outside the crew approves.** That person is a
  Hope Ambassador or a VWB admin.
- **Roles are enforced by the database,** not only by hiding buttons.

## The app itself

- **Simple view hides things. It never removes them.** People can always find a feature again.
- **Adults only (18+).**
- **No AI features in the app, and no AI art.** Simple shapes you draw yourself are fine,
  if they borrow nothing from other artists.
- **No marketing pages.** Work goes into the app.
- **Spark is the one reaction.** There are no likes or counts.
